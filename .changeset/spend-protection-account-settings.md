Settings, Your account has a Spend protection section: what every app follows unless it sets its own. Turn on pausing apps automatically (off by default), choose what pauses an app (a loop, at Relaxed, Standard or Strict, and on Workers Paid a spending cap per day, week or month), set a monthly budget for the whole bill including the $5 plan (Workers Paid), and choose how far above normal an hour must be before an unusual-usage alert. Each setting shows which apps it would act on right now: the apps looping, the apps over the cap, this month's bill against the budget and which app it would pause first, with links to the apps that set their own triggers or are never paused for the budget. Admins change these; members see them. Every change is recorded in the account's usage activity.
