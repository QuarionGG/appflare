Home has a new Usage section above Your apps. On Workers Paid it shows what this month's usage costs past what the $5 plan includes, the projected month with the plan, and what every app burns an hour now (red, naming the app, while one runs away). On Workers Free it shows what can be billed ($0, or what R2 bills past its free amount, which it does on every plan), the daily limit the account is closest to, and how many apps need a look. Below the totals, a product's last 7 days are stacked by app, one colour per app: whole days as an hourly average, then today hour by hour. Past six apps, the five that used the most this week keep their colours and the rest show together as Other apps. Without the permission to read Cloudflare's analytics, the section offers to reconnect instead.

Each app card shows a small chart of the app's last 7 days for its most telling product, in the app's colour (red while it runs away, grey while paused), with one line about it, such as its share of today's requests. A card leads with Pause while its app runs away or used up a free daily limit, and with Resume while it is paused, for admins. An app installed in the last hour with no usage yet says "Just installed" and "Usage shows up about an hour after install". The Resume dialog's "change it" link now opens the app's spend protection settings.
