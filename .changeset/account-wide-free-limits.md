On Workers Free, the daily limits now count the whole account. Cloudflare applies them to everything in the account, including Workers and databases Appflare did not install, and once something else uses one up, Appflare's apps fail too. Each check now also reads the account's total use today of every product with a daily limit, in the same request as the apps' usage, and uses it wherever a limit is judged or shown: the "free daily limit about to run out" and "used up" alerts and their Home rows, Home's closest daily limit, and the share of the free limit on an app's Usage tab. The part that is not from Appflare's apps shows as other usage in this account (not Appflare apps), and an alert gives the app's share of the account's total. Appflare keeps only today's total of that other usage: it is not charted, kept as history, capped or paused. Workers AI is counted for the whole account and shared by the apps that bind it, as before.

When the whole account has used 90% of a free daily limit, whatever used it, Home's Needs attention shows an account row and notification channels get a new event, Account near a free daily limit, once per limit and day: "This account has used 90% of today's free D1 rows written (90.3K of 100K), mostly other usage in this account. Cloudflare stops D1 for the whole account when it runs out, until 00:00 UTC." It names the Appflare app that used most of it when Appflare's apps did. A channel hears about a limit once a day, from whichever of the two comes first among the events it receives, so a channel that gets both is not told twice and a channel that gets only one always hears it. Existing channels that receive Free daily limit running out receive the new event too; untick it in a channel's settings if you do not want it.

The monthly budget now says what it counts: this month's Appflare usage, including the $5 plan. It never counted other Workers and resources in the account, so it is not a limit on the whole bill. A paused app's card on Home now says when it was paused ("Paused at 10:00 AM · no usage since", with the date for an earlier day), and a card's share of today's requests reads "under 1%" when tiny instead of "0%", and is left out when the app made none.
