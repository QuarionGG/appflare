An app's page has a Usage tab. It says in one line how the app is doing (normal, busier than usual, far above normal with the numbers, a free daily limit used up, paused and why, or just installed), with a banner and Pause app or Resume when it needs attention. A chart shows the product you pick over 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, with a line at the app's normal hour and markers for updates, settings changes, alerts and pauses. Under it, a table lists each product the app uses: the last 24 hours, today, this month, where the month is heading, how much of what the plan includes (or of the free limit) the account has used, and on Workers Paid the app's part of the cost past included. All money is an estimate at Cloudflare's list prices.

Spend protection for the app sits on the same tab: how far above normal an hour must be to alert, usage alerts written as a sentence ("When it has spent $6.00 this month", or on the Free plan a share of a free daily limit today) with where each stands, whether the app pauses itself on the account's triggers or its own, and, with the account's monthly budget on, a way to keep the budget from ever pausing the app. Every change is recorded in the app's Recent activity, with updates, alerts, pauses and resumes. A brand-new app says its usage shows up about 15 minutes after install and offers to turn on Workers Logs; a connection without the usage permission asks to reconnect Cloudflare. Members see everything and change nothing. The Resume dialog's "change it" link on Home now opens the app's spend protection.
